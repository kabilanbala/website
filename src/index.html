About CBSG

One partner for a wide range of building services.

CBSG Electromechanical Works LLC is a technical services company established in Dubai in 2018. We provide electromechanical, civil, renovation and day-to-day maintenance services, shaped around each client's requirements and satisfaction.

Our experienced team carries out MEP sub-contracting works, maintenance, renovation and related works with professionalism, quality and cost-effective solutions.

Our Services

Comprehensive services for your building and facility needs.

From essential building systems to finishing details, CBSG offers a broad range of services under one roof.

  • HVAC Systems

    Installation and maintenance of heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems, including various types of ducting works.

  • MEP Services

    Mechanical, electrical, plumbing, piping, and structured cabling.

  • Steel Fabrication

    Steel fabrication, stainless steel handrails, and other fabrication works.

  • Civil & Ceiling Works

    Ceiling works using 60 × 60 tiles, gypsum, and perforated aluminium systems.

  • Pavers, Tiles & Interlock

    Paver works, floor tiles, interlocking paving blocks, and kerbstones.

  • Partition Works

    Gypsum, aluminium, and fabric panel partitions.

  • Flooring Solutions

    Ceramic tiles, marble, carpet, parquet, PVC gymnasium flooring, and raised flooring.

  • Wall Covering & Cladding

    Wooden cladding, gypsum cladding, and aluminium cladding, including ALU-BOND.

  • Painting & Wall Finishing

    Internal and external wall painting, floor painting, epoxy flooring, wall tiling, and wallpaper fixing.

  • Woodwork & Joinery

    Wooden doors, windows, shelving, cabinets, cladding, and furniture.

  • Building & Office Cleaning

    Building cleaning and office cleaning services.
